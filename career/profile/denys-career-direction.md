# Denys — confirmed career direction

## Confirmed direction

Denys deliberately moved away from startup-title signaling and wants to build a **corporate path toward real organizational influence**.

The target is broader than team-level Tech Lead:

- substantive trust and judgment;
- system-level view across teams and domains;
- technical ownership of expensive/ambiguous problems;
- architecture and platform governance;
- influence without relying only on a formal title;
- eventual Staff / Principal / Solution or Enterprise Architect scope where supported by real responsibility.

## Current professional base

- Senior .NET/C# engineer; also works with Python and TypeScript.
- PhD.
- Current work context includes financial B2B SaaS, microservices, PostgreSQL, Wolverine, DDD, high-load, payments/integrations and AI-assisted engineering.
- User-stated compensation baseline in August 2026: about `$3.8k`; `$6k+` is a target/tail outcome, not assumed market median.

## Strategic constraint

Denys does not want a strategy built around artificial title progression, generic certificates or startup identity. The career case should demonstrate:

- sound architecture decisions and trade-offs;
- reliability in financial/distributed systems;
- cross-team leverage and governance;
- ability to reduce organizational uncertainty;
- trusted communication with technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Search constraints currently confirmed

- Senior / Team Lead / Tech Lead .NET backend;
- booking must be explicit or marked unconfirmed, never inferred;
