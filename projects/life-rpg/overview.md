# Life RPG validation project

Prototype/validation package for an AI-powered Life RPG that maps real-life habits and goals to quests, XP, stats, bosses and adaptive recovery mechanics.

## Current status

**Archived as an active startup direction.** On 2026-08-01 Denys stated that he had abandoned the startup focus in favor of a corporate career path with broader organizational influence.

The artifacts remain useful as:

- a product-validation case;
- a reusable content/landing/mockup pack;
- reference material for gamification and experimentation;
- evidence of autonomous digital asset production.

They are not an approved current business plan.

## Existing artifacts

Local root: `/root/life-rpg-validation`.

- `landing/` and `mockups/` — visual validation assets;
- `videos/` — rendered short-video assets;
- `validation-plan.md` — channels, thresholds and decision gates;
- `brand-positioning.md` — audience and messaging hypotheses;
- `reference-synthesis.md` — Habitica/Finch/Duolingo/RPG UI synthesis;
- `content-pack.md` — social/video drafts.

No completed validation report or verified evidence of a launched experiment was found during the 2026-08-16 inventory. Do not infer demand from the existence of assets.

## Core hypothesis retained for reference

> Users may care more about emotionally visible identity/progression than about another flat habit tracker.

The proposed validation correctly distinguishes “cool idea” reactions from stronger evidence such as waitlist conversion, unprompted beta requests and concrete use cases.
