2. Do not imply availability for a second full-time role. Apply only to bounded part-time/advisory work with explicit hours and no domain conflict.
3. Prepare a tailored one-page profile: payments/microservices ownership, DDD, high-load, observability, AI-development process, Airflow/ETL and integrations.
