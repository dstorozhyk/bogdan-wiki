## Scan completeness

The direct DOU RSS and DefTech listing requests returned Cloudflare 403 responses. Feed2JSON recovered the booking, Senior-remote and Team-Lead-remote lists with valid `content_html` long enough to classify every DOU candidate without redundant per-vacancy Jina requests; the content exposed 11 booking, 25 Senior-remote and 3 Team-Lead-remote items.[6][7][8]

Jina Reader returned validated live content for both the full DefTech listing and the `.NET`-filtered listing. The seven `.NET` cards were all obvious level, office, ERP, QA or desktop mismatches after reconciliation with the full DOU feed records, so no DefTech survivor required another Jina vacancy request; no rate limit was encountered.[6][9]

Djinni .NET 5+ and Tech Leadership 5+ were scanned with the mobilisation-reservation filter; the only reservation-tagged .NET result was hybrid, capped at $3000 and required B1 English, reservation-tagged leadership results were non-.NET and/or office/hybrid, and the strongest otherwise-nearby .NET result stated Intermediate/B1 English in its full page.[10][11]

LinkedIn's guest endpoints returned concrete public listings for both Senior .NET and .NET Tech Lead Ukraine remote searches. Full descriptions produced one additional shortlist item—the Ciklum payments Lead role—while other plausible results were rejected for mandatory English, frontend/full-stack emphasis, IoT/desktop mismatch, missing fully-remote proof, or duplication across searches and DOU.[12][13]
